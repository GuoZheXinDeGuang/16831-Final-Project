## Choices made (and why)
- **Environment: FourRoom Distractors** (not AntMaze): pure-torch rendering (no MuJoCo/EGL), env-native success
  (distance < 16 px), cheap exact state restoration for branching.
- **Model/planner: H-JEPA 2-level + official `fourroom_l2` planner** (paper: 81.3 ± 6.7 %). Level-2 GD (800 samples,
  horizon 12 decaying) emits a level-2 latent subgoal; level-1 GD MPC (100 samples, horizon 2 blocks × 5 steps) tracks it;
  replanning every 10 env steps. The 2-level model has more episode failures than L3 (96 %), leaving room for P2/P3.
- **Determinism fix (deviation from upstream, documented):** upstream FourRoom resets are *unseeded* (tasks have no
  `seed` column), and two runs of the official `eval.py` gave different step counts (task 0: 168 vs 141 steps).
  `run_baseline.py` seeds env resets with `env_seed = 100000 + task` and enables torch deterministic algorithms.
  Planner seeding is identical to upstream chunked eval (`seed + task_index`). Result: **bit-identical** trajectories,
  subgoals and distractor motion across runs and across concurrent processes (checked; also `tests/test_p0_smoke.py`).

### Attempt / label definition
- One **attempt**: restore the env snapshot of baseline planning call t, freeze the level-2 latent subgoal g the
  baseline gave its level-1 MPC at t, run **only** the frozen level-1 MPC (upstream GradientSolver + LevelCostModel,
  baseline plan config, replanning every 10 steps) towards g for 30 steps, encoding every real frame.
- Controller conditions: budget B ∈ {B0 = 2 GD iters × 4 samples, B1 = 10 × 20, B2 = 90 × 100 (= baseline, early-stops
  at ~30)}; execution horizon H ∈ {5, 10, 20, 30} env steps (prefixes of the same rollout).
- **Y = 1 iff min_{k≤H} ||enc2(o_{t+k}) − g||² ≤ ε**, ε = 6.70 = median latent distance between real observations
  within 8 px of each other, computed on the TRAIN split only (8 px ≈ 3 agent steps; half the env's 16 px goal radius).
  Sensitivity: ε at 4 px → 32–39 % positives, 8 px → 52–60 %, 16 px → 77–79 %.
- Inputs at inference: z_t (level-2 latent of the current frame), g, H, B only. xy is used for calibration/analysis only.
- Stored per attempt: z1/z2_t, g, H, B, the full distance curve, xy trajectory, env-goal flag, steps to reach, wall clock,
  level-1 predictor calls/rows per solve.

## Known limitations / caveats
- One model seed (42) and one planner seed for P0. The paper averages 3 seeds.
- The predictor was trained only on subgoals the baseline *selected*. P2 will score other level-2 candidates
  (distribution shift); collect attempts on non-selected candidates before relying on it for reranking.
- Labels are latent-threshold based (ε calibrated on train). ε-sensitivity is reported above; no xy-probe label yet.
- Wall-clock numbers were measured under GPU sharing. Predictor call counts are the comparable compute measure.

## P2 / P3 — selection and repair: DONE

- P2 rerank: argmin cost − λ·c_min·log(R+0.05) over all 800 level-2 candidates (one extra batched rollout per call).
- P3 trigger: 3 consecutive subgoal misses (latent, ε) or goal-latent distance not improved 5 % in 5 calls; 3-call hold, 1-call cooldown.
- P3 repair: level-2 GD re-solve with −λ_R·c_min·log R(z, ĝ1) (differentiable MLP) + β·c_min·visited-region penalty;
  fallback = full replan if repaired R < 0.3. B4 = same trigger, plain re-solve. Ablation = repair with λ_R = 0, no rerank.
- Selected on val (20 tasks): λ = 0.25 (tie with 0.5 broken by steps), β = 20, λ_R = 10.

**Mechanism (test call logs; repaired-subgoal similarity to visited latents, 0 = novel, 1 = visited, median):**
β = 50 0.019, β = 20 0.025, β = 0 0.338, original Ours (λ_R = 10) 0.320, λ_R = 1 0.032 but 79% of repairs vetoed by the
R ≥ 0.3 check and replaced by a plain replan. So: (1) novelty is the active ingredient: without it repair = baseline, and
B4 (re-solve on the same trigger) = baseline; (2) a distance-like R undoes it in every form tried: strong λ_R pulls the
repaired subgoal back to the visited region (as non-novel as β = 0), weak λ_R lets novelty win but the R gate then vetoes
the novel subgoal, the expected-cost form is indifferent to progress whenever R is low (which a distance prior says for
every novel subgoal), and reranking with R picks near-current-state subgoals every call. Online miss memory does not help.
Conclusion: in AntMaze, "reachable" (by a learned predictor) and "useful for escaping a stall" point in opposite directions.
